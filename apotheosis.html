Concept
North Star
Patience rewarded with violence.

Apotheosis is a top-down action game about a broken god who can only feel anger. The world's nine emotional currents, the Navra, were surgically severed from reality and bound into nine Wardens: monument-like beings that now embody what the world has lost. Rah-uh, who retained only Krodha (Anger), is the only Broken God capable of forward motion. Every other emotion is paralysed by what it is. Anger moves.

Combat is built around a single truth: the player is never overwhelmed, only undisciplined. Every encounter is a puzzle of observation and timing. The player studies an enemy, finds the opening, and commits with contained, explosive precision. Skill expression lives in the Goad: a mechanic that rewards patience with the most violent payoff in the game. Master it, and you become the apex predator.

Project Highlight
The Goad System
The Goad is not a button press. It is the automatic resolution of a declared stance when conditions are met. Skill is expressed in creating those conditions, not in the execution. Three components form one interlocked mechanism.
01
Dodge Field
The Interface
Hold (X) to declare readiness. Active for as long as the input is held with Composure remaining. The player retains full movement while the field is active. No movement occurs automatically. The player simply waits, positioned.
02
Goad
The Outcome
When the Dodge Field detects an incoming enemy hitbox, the Goad fires instantly: a 5-10 frame invincible dash in the last inputted direction. Zero startup frames, zero recovery. The enemy is compelled forward in its attack chain. Goading the final chain attack breaks the enemy.
03
Composure
The Cost
The meter governing how long the Dodge Field can be held and how many Goads can execute before the player must recover. Drains passively while the field is active; takes a flat hit per Goad. At zero, the Dodge Field cannot activate and the player enters the Broken State.
Combat Design 1
Purely skill-gated
The only cost of a Goad attempt was timing. Between Goads, no productive action was available. The player waited while the enemy recovered. Patience was the only tool, and waiting was dead time.
Combat Design 2 (Current)
Timing skill + resource discipline
The player must consider not only whether they can Goad, but whether they can afford to. Attacking composed enemies recovers Composure, making every moment between Goads productive. Waiting is no longer the only option, and spending Composure is a genuine commitment.
The Composure Meter creates a structural mirror: the player who runs out of Composure becomes, briefly, exactly what they were trying to create in their enemies. Broken. Incapacitated. Unable to act. The same rules that govern the arena apply to everyone in it. This symmetry reinforces the core identity of Apotheosis: the player is not above the encounter. They are part of it.
