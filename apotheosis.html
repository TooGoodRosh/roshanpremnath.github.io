The Game
Top-down action game
Play as Ra-hu, a god cast into the mortal realm and stripped of all but one emotion — fury. Fight your way through various regions, each overseen by a warden of emotion, to reclaim your god-hood.

Bide your time, coerce your foes into vulnerability, and vanquish them in a single decisive blow.
