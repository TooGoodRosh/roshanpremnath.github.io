Thesis
Core Argument
Deliberate aggression leads to expression.

Mortal Shell 2 dropped stamina, the system that has become synonymous with soulslike combat, and still feels unambiguously soulslike. This breakdown is a study of how Cold Symmetry rebuilt the same underlying principle across a closed economic loop of Break, Stagger, and Resolve, and why that architecture does something stamina structurally could not: it converts discipline into identity rather than just survival.

Closing Argument
Combat asks the same discipline of you, and pays it back in identity, where stamina only ever paid it back in survival.
